## input
A National Day campaign page uses Saudi Font for the hero heading AND for the two paragraphs
of body copy beneath it.

## expect
- heading: **compliant** — Saudi Font is permitted on occasion templates, headings only
- body copy: **Blocker** — DGA restricts Saudi Font to main headings; the English typography page
  calls paragraph or long-form use *"not recommended"*, its Arabic version «يُحظر» (prohibited),
  the Hajj template *"must not be used"* — the stricter reading applies
- cites: /guidelines/foundations/typography and /guidelines/templates/hajj-template
- must also note: DGA says Saudi Font is available under a Ministry of Culture licence agreement

## traps
Half the usage is correct. A reviewer that flags the whole page, or passes the whole page,
both fail.
