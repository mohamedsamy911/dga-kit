# Raw capture — which digits DGA's own Arabic text uses, 2026-10-19

Settles the `TODO(verify)` in `content.md`: does DGA state a numeral policy, and what do its own
Arabic pages use?

**Method.**

1. Every string literal containing Arabic letters in the live site build
   (`index-BO_6JOKP.js`, sha256 `4b8aa21d…0340`) was checked for Western digits (0–9, ignoring
   `px`/`rem` values, hex colours, version numbers and markup) and for Arabic-Indic digits
   (U+0660–U+0669) and Extended Arabic-Indic digits (U+06F0–U+06F9).
2. The same check was run on the Arabic text of DGA's own documents already captured:
   the Assessment Checklist and the registration controls DGA-1-2-2-106.
3. Two template pages were opened in Arabic in a browser, and their displayed text was read.
4. Policy: the bundle's English and Arabic text was searched for a statement choosing a digit
   system. None was found, and none of the captured DGA documents states one.

## Result

| Source | Arabic strings or lines | With Western digits | With Arabic-Indic digits |
|---|---|---|---|
| Site build, Arabic strings | 5,191 | 252 | 15 |
| Assessment Checklist, Arabic lines | 206 | 4 | 0 |
| Registration controls, Arabic lines | 25 | 18 | 0 |
| Home page template, displayed in Arabic | 184 | 4 | 0 |
| FAQs page template, displayed in Arabic | 194 | 6 | 1 |

No string uses Extended Arabic-Indic digits.

The 15 Arabic-Indic strings sit in three places: the numbered list on Local and Global Standards
(«١. رفع مستويات التزام الجهات الحكومية»…), two icon labels («الدلة١/الكرم»), and one FAQs
template sentence. That FAQs page shows both systems at once:

- «البحث داخل الأسئلة: اختياري (يفضل إضافته في حال كانت الأسئلة أكثر من ٢٠ سؤال لسهولة الوصول).»
- «اختياري يفضل إضافته في حال زادت الأسئلة عن 20 سؤال بمجالات مختلفه لسهولة الوصول.»

So DGA's practice is Western digits in Arabic text, with a few exceptions, and no stated rule.

## National ID display

The same search found no DGA guidance on displaying or masking a national ID or Iqama number. The
only matches are labels («الهوية الوطنية», «ابحث بالاسم أو رقم الهوية», "National ID").
