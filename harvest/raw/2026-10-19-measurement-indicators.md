# Raw capture — DGA's two measurement indicators (DXMI 2026, Qiyas 2025)

The launch gate names two DGA measurement regimes it could not read: the **Digital Experience
Maturity Index** and the **Digital Transformation Measurement** (قياس التحول الرقمي). These are the
public reports for each, downloaded by the owner on 2026-10-19 in a browser (dga.gov.sa challenges
automated clients). Neither report publishes its item-level criteria.

### Recommendations the kit cites (pp. 64–67)

The report gives 14 recommendations, *"directed at entities that own digital platforms"*. Four
touch a platform's interface:

<!-- dga -->
> 3. Simplifying login and authentication paths: Highlighting the login point clearly and
> independently within the main browsing bar, and reducing intermediate steps, redirections, and
> pop-up windows. And making login via the "Unified National Access" (Nafath) a primary option
> whenever applicable, to reduce the effort required to access services and begin executing them.
<!-- /dga -->

<!-- dga -->
> 4. Enhancing Responsiveness, Compatibility, and Interface Integrity: Applying responsive design
> principles across various screens and devices, and addressing instances of visual overlapping or
> breaking. This includes correcting the programmatic logic for input validation mechanisms and
> aligning form attributes, relying on accessibility that is fundamentally embedded within the
> platform's architecture, and dispensing with unstable floating external accessibility add-ons
> (Accessibility Overlays), to ensure reliable digital environment that supports accessibility for
> all beneficiaries.
<!-- /dga -->

<!-- dga -->
> 5. Developing search and information retrieval: Providing smart and effective search
> capabilities, especially in service- and content-heavy platforms, with an instant response that
> recognizes spelling variations and synonyms and supports advanced filtering. This is alongside
> the visual highlighting of matching words and presenting results within a clear and readable
> structure, while ensuring the programmatic announcement of the match to be articulated by
> assistive technologies and avoiding reliance on color alone. It also involves announcing the
> number of results via a live region (aria-live) and semantically linking the highlighted word to
> be recognized by screen readers, to accelerate the access of all beneficiary segments to the
> required service or information.
<!-- /dga -->

<!-- dga -->
> 7. Increasing compliance with digital inclusion requirements: Developing digital channels in
> accordance with the nationally approved version of the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines
> (WCAG), with a compliance level of no less than "AA", such as: sufficient color contrast,
> displaying the visual focus indicator, providing a skip to main content link, logical ordering of
> headings, providing alternative texts for images, and the programmatic linking of form fields to
> their labels. In addition to providing documents and content in accessible formats, supporting
> assistive technologies -including screen reading programs, magnification tools, keyboard
> navigation, and electronic Braille displays- providing accessible alternatives for verification
> mechanisms (CAPTCHA) including the audio alternative, and combining automated and actual testing
> while incorporating testing by persons with disabilities and seniors.
<!-- /dga -->

**Method — and why nothing below is fenced:** the PDF's Arabic extracts in visual order (each line

- **What it measures (p. 7):** government **entities**, not individual platforms — 10 perspectives
  (مناظير), 23 axes (محاور), 95 criteria (معايير). One perspective, مركزية المستفيد (beneficiary
  centricity), has the axes مشاركة المستفيد and تجربة المستفيد; another, القنوات والخدمات, has
  جودة الخدمات الرقمية and القنوات والخدمات الرقمية.
- **The 95 criteria are not in this report.** Entities answer them in DGA's measurement system
  (نظام قياس), with evidence that DGA's team verifies (p. 4).
- **Compliance levels per criterion (p. 8):** full (all of a criterion's requirements met),
  partial (one requirement missed), none (two or more missed).
- **Who was measured (p. 8):** 240 government entities; 11 measuring for the first time were not
  counted in the report's results, leaving 229.

## What these two reports change for the kit

Neither regime is a per-platform launch check that the kit could score: Qiyas measures the
entity, and DXMI assesses a selected set of platforms each year with criteria shared only with the
owning entities. The launch gate can name them and say what they measure; it still cannot score
them.
